Classic sponge cake.

Preheat the oven to 180 degrees Celsius and line two round tins with baking paper. Beat 200 grams of soft butter with 200 grams of caster sugar until pale and fluffy.

Add four eggs one at a time, then fold in 200 grams of self-raising flour. Divide the batter between the tins and bake for 25 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.

Let the sponges cool completely before filling them with raspberry jam and whipped cream. The cake keeps for two days in an airtight container.
